A FEW DETAILS

Before you
try Scoop.

What kind of changes does Scoop look for?

Leadership changes, company initiatives, expansion, partnerships and deal risks that are relevant to your offer. Availability depends on the account’s public footprint and the quality of the evidence.

Does a signal mean an account wants to buy?

No. A public change can give you a reason to investigate or ask a question. It does not establish budget or buying intent.

Is Salesforce integration available today?

It is planned for the team product. The first pilot uses a CSV account list and email delivery. We’ll confirm integration scope and timing with each pilot team.

How does the pilot work?

The proposed 30-day pilot costs €150 excluding VAT for up to five users and 50 pooled accounts. It includes onboarding, four reviewed briefs and a results review before you decide whether to continue.

What if nothing relevant happens at an account?

We would rather report no verified relevant change than fill the brief with unsupported or unrelated news.
